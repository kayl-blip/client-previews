01 / Lumbar spine, San Jose

Non-surgical low back pain treatment in San Jose.

Low back pain has more than one cause, and the causes are not treated the same way. We test yours first, with digital X-ray on site where it is indicated, and nerve testing when the pain travels into your hip or leg. Treatment here is non-surgical and drug-free.

02 / What it is and what causes it

Low back pain is a symptom. The cause is what we are looking for.

Most low back pain starts in one of a few places. Muscle and ligament strain. The small joints at the back of the spine. Or a disc, the cushion that sits between two vertebrae, bulging or herniated and pressing on a nerve root.

Pain that stays in the back behaves differently from pain that runs into the buttock, thigh or foot, and that difference changes what we do about it.

If your pain began after a car accident, take it seriously even if the impact seemed minor at the time. Soft-tissue injuries often surface days later, as inflammation builds.

03 / How we assess it

We measure the injury before we treat it.

Your visit starts with your history and a physical exam: how the pain moves, what makes it worse, where strength, reflexes and sensation have changed.

Digital X-ray

Where it is indicated we take high-frequency digital X-ray on site, which shows bone, alignment and the spacing between vertebrae. What an X-ray cannot show you is how a nerve is behaving.

Nerve testing

So if the pain runs past your buttock into the thigh, calf or foot, we add nerve testing in the same visit. Electromyography (EMG) reads the electrical activity in a muscle. Nerve conduction velocity testing times the signal moving along the nerve that supplies it.

What it settles

Between them we can tell whether a nerve is genuinely involved, or whether the pain is being referred from elsewhere.

04 / How it is treated

A plan built on your findings, not on a template.

What we recommend depends on what the exam and the testing show.

If a disc is the problem, one option for the lower back is non-surgical spinal decompression: a traction-based table treatment that gradually unloads the segment. Hands-on chiropractic care works on the stiff joints and the guarded muscle around them. None of it involves surgery or a prescription.

Before anything begins we take you through the treatment and its risks, and you decide. Some people ease off inside a few weeks and others take longer. Individual results vary by person and by cause. If you are not responding, we say so and change course.

06 / When to seek care sooner

Some back pain should not wait for an appointment.

Emergency signs

Go to an emergency room now, or call 911, if you lose control of your bladder or bowel, if you have numbness through the groin or inner thighs, if a leg is quickly getting weaker, or if the pain arrives with a fever. The same goes after a hard fall or a serious collision. Those signs call for a hospital and imaging on the spot, not a chiropractic exam.

Emergency medicine and what we do are two different jobs. An emergency room rules out what is life threatening and treats the pain that day. Our work starts with what has not been tested yet.

If our exam turns up something we cannot image here, we refer you out for MRI immediately.
