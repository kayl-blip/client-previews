After a collision

The extra paperwork after a collision

Two of the forms above carry more weight once a claim is open. The auto accident intake records the mechanics of the impact and the hours after it, which is the detail people lose once a few weeks have passed. The authorization to release records is what lets us send your examination and testing report to your attorney or to the adjuster handling the claim. Nothing leaves this office without it.

We accept liens. That means care can go ahead with nothing paid by you while the claim is open, if you qualify. Questions about the claim itself belong with your attorney. We keep the clinical record.

Without a printer

If you have no way to print them

You do not need a printer. Paper copies of every form sit at the front desk and you can complete them when you arrive. Doing it on the day adds to your appointment, which is the only reason we ask you to do it in advance. Arrive if you are filling them in here. If holding a pen is difficult because of your injury, say so when you book.
