01 / Accessibility statement

Effective date:

Last updated:

This site should work for you, however you are reading it.

Many people land here after a car accident, so you may be on a phone, one-handed, with a headache, or using a screen reader. This page states the standard we build to, what is already in place, and where we know we still fall short. If something here blocks you, tell us, and we will fix it.

02 / In place today

What is already in place

  • 01

    Every page can be operated with a keyboard alone, and the focus outline stays visible so you can see where you are on the page.

  • 02

    Text and background colours are checked against the contrast levels in the standard below.

  • 03

    Every image carries alt text that describes what it actually shows.

  • 04

    Buttons and links are sized to be hit with a thumb.

  • 05

    Pinch-zoom is not switched off anywhere on this site. Some clinic websites disable it, which stops you enlarging text on a phone, and we do not.

  • 06

    Pages do not scroll sideways at any screen width.

  • 07

    Phone numbers are tap-to-call links rather than pictures of numbers.

03 / The standard

The standard we target, and where we fall short

We build to the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) version 2.2, Level AA. WCAG is the international reference standard for making web content usable by people with disabilities. We aim at it and we test against it as the site changes. We do not claim a perfect score.

Known gaps today:.

Parts of this site come from other providers, including the appointment scheduler and the embedded map, and those parts are not fully under our control. Where we find a barrier in one, we report it to that provider and look for a route around it.

04 / Reporting route

Tell us what blocked you

  • 01

    If any part of this site is hard or impossible for you to use, we want to hear about it. Call (408) 292-2225 and say you are calling about the website. [UNCONFIRMED]

  • 02

    It helps if you can tell us which page you were on, what you were trying to do, and what you were using to read it, for example a screen reader, voice control, or a phone set to large text. You do not need to know the technical name for the problem. Describing what happened is enough.

  • 03

    If you need something from this site in another format, ask us on the call.
