01 / Corrective chiropractic, San Jose

Chiropractic care in San Jose, for injuries and for pain that has not settled

We provide corrective chiropractic care and trauma rehabilitation, which is care aimed at getting function back after an injury such as a car accident. Treatment is non-surgical and drug-free. We examine and test first, then treat what the findings show.

02 / Two kinds of patient

Who it is for

Two kinds of patient come here. The first was in a collision and is now dealing with neck and shoulder pain, stiffness, a head that will not turn as far as it used to, or pain that has spread since the day it happened. The second has had neck pain, back pain or headaches for months, has rested it, and is no better for resting. Reduced range of motion is worth taking seriously in both groups, because it can be measured and it is usually what limits work and sleep. If you are not sure which group you are in, that is what the evaluation is for.

  • 01after a crashStiffness, lost movement, or pain that has spread since the day it happened
  • 02months onPain that rest has not settled, in the neck, the back or the head

03 / Assessment first, then the plan

How it works

Assessment comes first. We take your history, watch how you move, measure your range of motion, and test where testing is indicated. Only then do we build a plan. The plan usually combines two things. Adjustments, meaning a controlled movement applied to a specific joint to restore motion in it. And physical medicine, meaning treatment applied to the injured soft tissue to reduce pain and rebuild the strength that supports the joint. Care is staged. The first stage targets pain and inflammation, the next targets movement, the last targets holding the gain. Every stage ends in a re-examination rather than rolling on indefinitely.

What the plan combines

AdjustmentsA controlled movement, one joint at a time
Physical medicineApplied to the injured tissue itself
SequencePain, then movement, then holding it

04 / Visits, tapering, review points

What a course of care looks like

01

The evaluation

The first visit is an evaluation. History, examination, testing where it is indicated, then a plain explanation of what we found and what we propose. Nothing gets treated before that conversation.

02

Visits, tapering and review

From there, visits run at a set frequency that tapers as you improve, with a re-examination at each review point. We compare each re-examination against your starting measurements. If they are not moving, the plan changes, or we refer you to someone better placed to help. Individual results vary, and we will not tell you at the outset how many visits your recovery will take.

Start with the examination. You leave knowing what we found, what we propose, and what it will take. Call (408) 292-2225 [UNCONFIRMED] or book online.

05 / Digital X-ray and nerve testing

How we confirm it is the right treatment

High-frequency digital X-ray is performed here, on site, in the same visit. It shows alignment, joint spacing, fracture and degenerative change, which is what we want to see before anyone puts hands on a spine. Digital X-ray is common across San Jose clinics, and on its own it settles very little. The nerve testing beside it is less common. Where your symptoms suggest a nerve is involved, we can measure that here with nerve conduction velocity testing and electromyography (EMG), which record how fast a nerve is conducting a signal and how the muscle it supplies responds. Testing on site is what lets us treat the finding rather than the guess.

What each one reads

  1. Digital X-rayWhat the bones and the joint spaces look like
  2. Conduction velocityHow quickly a nerve carries its signal
  3. ElectromyographyHow the muscle that nerve supplies responds

06 / The emergency room boundary

What chiropractic care does not do

This is not emergency care. Straight after a collision an emergency department is the right first stop. Their job is to rule out the injuries that can kill you, and to treat you for pain. That is a different job from ours, and it is the one that has to happen first. What an emergency visit does not usually include is testing for the soft-tissue and nerve damage that turns into a long-term problem, and that is the part we pick up afterwards. Beyond that, we do not prescribe medication, we do not perform surgery, and we do not promise to make any condition go away for good. Where a specialist is the right answer, we refer you to one.

Outside our scope

  • Emergency care
  • Prescribing medication
  • Surgery
  • A promised cure

07 / Headache, neck, back

Conditions this care addresses

Chiropractic care is used to treat headaches that originate in the neck, including cervicogenic headache, which is the term for a headache generated by the joints and soft tissue of the upper neck rather than by the head itself. Results vary by person and by cause, and some headaches have causes this care cannot address. Neck and shoulder pain, back pain, and pain that started with a collision are the other common reasons patients are here.

  1. CervicogenicGenerated in the upper joints, felt in the head
  2. Neck and shoulderPain and lost movement, often post-impact

08 / Before your first visit

Common questions

How soon can I be seen?

Same-day appointments are usually available [UNCONFIRMED]. Call the clinic at (408) 292-2225 [UNCONFIRMED] and ask for the next opening.

Is treatment painful?

Treatment is non-surgical and drug-free. We explain the risks before care begins, and if something hurts during a visit, say so and we stop.

The emergency room said nothing was broken. Why am I still sore?

An emergency department checks for the injuries that need emergency treatment, and that is a different question from what your soft tissue and nerves are doing. If pain, stiffness or numbness has appeared or worsened since that visit, get examined.

Do I have to keep coming forever?

No. Care is staged, and each stage ends in a re-examination, so there is a defined point at which we discharge you, change the plan, or refer you on. Individual results vary.

What will it cost?

The consultation and evaluation is offered at no cost to you. Coverage for treatment depends on your policy and your case, and we check yours before you start.
