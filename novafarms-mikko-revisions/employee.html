It all began with a group of friends, a shared dream, and the sweat it took to build some of the first and largest outdoor grows in New England, where we continue to set the standards for products.

Our team’s dedication to the plant, the craft and to our people has led us to become the Nova Farms you know today— a leading regional purveyor with farms and operations in seven communities across five states and growing.

We’re proud to bring you an array of wonderful products, love and respect for the cannabis community, and an unbridled sense of optimism for our shared future.
