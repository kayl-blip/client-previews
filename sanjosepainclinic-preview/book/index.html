02 / What the form asks

Start with the form.

The form asks for your name, phone, email and the times you can come in. One question asks whether a collision was involved. Answer yes and four more fields open: the date of the collision, your insurer, your adjuster if one has been assigned, and your attorney if you have one. Give us what you have and leave the rest blank. Those answers tell us which intake, insurance and auto-accident forms to send you to complete before the visit. They also tell us how much time to hold, so your examination and your testing happen on site in one visit rather than across three appointments and an outside referral.

04 / After you send it

What happens next.

You will get a confirmation with the time, the address and the forms to fill in beforehand. Complete them at home if you can. It is easier than working through a clipboard in a waiting room with a sore neck.

Bring the completed forms and anything you already hold from the collision, such as the claim number and your adjuster's or attorney's contact details.

Allow for the appointment. We will tell you what we found, what we recommend and what it costs before any care starts. What we recommend depends on what the examination and the testing show, and individual results vary.
