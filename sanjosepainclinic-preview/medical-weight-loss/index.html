Medically supervised weight loss in San Jose

02 / Who it is for

Who the program is for

Our medically supervised weight loss program is for adults who want the work overseen by a clinician rather than run alone. That includes people who have lost weight before and regained it, people whose progress has stalled, and people whose weight is loading a back, hip or knee problem we are already treating. Supervision means someone is measuring what is happening and adjusting the plan when the numbers move, or when they do not. If your weight change points to a medical cause that belongs with your physician, we will say so and refer rather than enrol you. Results vary from person to person.

03 / The sequence

How the program works

01

Intake

It starts with an intake: your history, what you have already tried, your current medication, and what you are trying to change.

02

Bioimpedance baseline

We then take a bioimpedance assessment, which passes a very low-level electrical current through the body to estimate the proportion of fat, lean tissue and water you are carrying. That matters, because two people at the same weight can be carrying very different amounts of muscle, and because losing lean tissue is not a result worth having.

03

The plan

The plan is built from that baseline.

04

Every review

At each review the same measurement is repeated, and the plan changes to match the trend rather than the day's number.

Results vary from person to person.

04 / Before enrolment

What we check before you start

Screening record

Assessment precedes treatment

010203

We assess before we treat, in this program as in any other. History and body composition come first. Where weight is aggravating a spine or joint problem, we examine that directly, and high-frequency digital X-ray is on site if imaging is indicated, so it happens in the same visit instead of at a separate appointment somewhere else. Imaging is not a routine part of a weight loss plan, and we do not order it as one. We use it only where a musculoskeletal finding calls for it. If your history points to a condition that belongs with your physician, we refer rather than work around it.

  • First history and body composition
  • Then direct examination where weight loads a spine or joint
  • Only if indicated high-frequency digital X-ray, on site
  • Where it belongs elsewhere referral to your physician

05 / Between reviews

What a course of care looks like

The first visit is an assessment, not a sale. We take the history, measure the baseline, and explain the plan and what it costs before you commit to anything. After that, reviews are scheduled at set intervals, and each one repeats the same measurements so the comparison is honest. Between reviews you have a defined plan to follow and a way to reach us if something is not working. If the trend is flat across two consecutive reviews, we change the approach or tell you plainly that this program is not the right tool for you. Outcomes differ from one person to the next, and so does the time any change takes.

06 / Refusals

What this program does not do

We do not promise an amount of weight or a date by which you will lose it. Individual results vary, and any program that puts a number on your outcome before it has measured you is guessing. This is not treatment for an eating disorder, and it is not a substitute for the care your physician provides for conditions such as diabetes, thyroid disease or heart disease.

It does not cure any condition. We do not publish before and after figures, because we do not hold sourced ones, and invented ones would tell you nothing about your own case. If the program is not appropriate for you, we will say so at the assessment.

07 / Asked often

Common questions

How soon can I start?

Same-day appointments are usually available. [UNCONFIRMED] Call and we will tell you what is open today.

Does the program involve prescription medication?

You will be told exactly what the plan includes, in writing, at the assessment.

How is progress measured?

By repeating the body composition measurement, not by the scale alone. A scale cannot tell you whether the weight you lost came from fat or from muscle, and that difference matters.

Is it covered by insurance?

Weight loss programs are often not covered by health insurance, and we cannot promise that any insurer will pay. We tell you the cost before you start so there is no surprise later.
