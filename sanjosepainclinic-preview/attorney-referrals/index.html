For personal injury attorneys / San Jose

Auto injury care on a lien, documented with measured nerve findings

We take auto accident and whiplash referrals from San Jose personal injury attorneys and work the client up in one visit. Whiplash (a rapid neck acceleration-deceleration injury) is examined, imaged and nerve-tested on site, using electromyography (EMG), nerve conduction velocity and diagnostic vascular studies: tests that measure how a nerve or a vessel is functioning rather than describe how the patient says it feels. 01

Intake and examination

What your client gets clinically

One appointment, one complete work-up

Your client is examined, imaged and nerve-tested in a single visit at our San Jose clinic. All primary testing is performed on site, so there is no second trip to an outside facility before findings exist, and no waiting period in the record while an outside test is scheduled. Where findings point beyond our scope, we refer out promptly, for MRI or for a specialist opinion, and the referral and the reason for it are recorded in the file. Treatment is non-surgical and drug-free. Individual results vary, and we do not forecast a recovery timeline before the examination is done.

02

Objective testing

The objective findings you receive

Measured values, not a pain score

Three tests carry this.

  • Channel 01 / muscle activity

    Electromyography (EMG) records the electrical activity in a muscle and shows whether the nerve feeding that muscle is working normally.

  • Channel 02 / signal speed

    Nerve conduction velocity measures how fast a signal travels along a nerve and reports it as a number, which can be compared left against right and repeated later to show change over time.

  • Channel 03 / blood flow

    Diagnostic vascular studies measure blood flow where a collision may have compromised it.

All three run on site, in the same visit as the examination. In a review of 53 published pages across six competing San Jose auto-injury websites, August 2026, none of them advertised any of the three.

We report what the tests measured. We do not opine on liability or on case value. More detail is on our on-site diagnostics page.

03

The report

Documentation format and turnaround

What the report contains, and when you get it

Documented injury report

Signed by the treating clinician

The report carries the collision history as your client gives it, the examination findings, any imaging performed, the measured values from nerve and vascular testing, the working diagnosis, the treatment plan and the clinical reasoning behind it.

It is written to be read by an adjuster and by a reviewing physician, so the measured values sit alongside the subjective account rather than replacing it.
