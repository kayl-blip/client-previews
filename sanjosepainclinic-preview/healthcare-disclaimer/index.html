03 / The clauses

01General information, not medical advice

Everything written here describes conditions and testing in general terms. Your injury is specific to you and can only be assessed by examining you.

02Reading this site does not make you a patient

A treatment relationship starts when you are seen and accepted for care at the clinic, not when you read a page or submit a form.

03Emergencies

If you think you may be having a medical emergency, call 911 or go to the nearest emergency department. Emergency medicine does a different job than we do: it rules out what is immediately dangerous and treats it. Testing and documenting soft-tissue and nerve injury is the job that comes after.

04Individual results vary

Any outcome described anywhere on this site is a general description of what care aims to do, not a prediction for you. Results vary by person, by injury and by how soon care begins.

05Scope of practice and licence

This is a non-surgical, drug-free practice. We do not perform surgery and we do not prescribe medication. This section will name the treating clinician and their licence.

06What our on-site testing does, and what it does not do

Electromyography (EMG) measures the electrical activity in a muscle. Nerve conduction velocity testing measures how fast a signal travels along a nerve. Diagnostic vascular studies look at blood flow. These tests produce measured findings. A finding is evidence, not a diagnosis on its own and not a forecast of how you will recover.

07Insurance, liens and billing

What is covered depends on your policy, your case and the insurer. Nothing on this site is a promise that any particular treatment will be paid for.

08We do not give legal advice

We can explain how a lien or a letter of protection works as a billing mechanism. We do not advise you on whether to sign anything, whether to settle, or what a claim is worth. Those are questions for a personal-injury attorney.

09Reviews and information published by other platforms

Ratings and reviews shown or linked from this site are published by third parties, are not written or edited by us, and reflect one person's experience rather than a typical result.
