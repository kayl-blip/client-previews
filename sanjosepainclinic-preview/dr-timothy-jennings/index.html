02 / Credentials on file

Six board certifications

Board certification means an outside body has examined and credentialed the practitioner in a named discipline. Dr. Jennings holds six.

  • Applied kinesiology

    Board Certified Applied Kinesiologist, International College of Applied Kinesiology (I.C.A.K.). Applied kinesiology uses manual muscle testing as part of the physical examination.

  • Clinical proficiency

    Clinically Proficient, International College of Applied Kinesiology (I.C.A.K.).

  • Pain management

    Diplomate in Pain Management, American Academy of Integrative Medicine (A.A.I.M.).

  • Clinical nutrition

    Diplomate in Clinical Nutrition, American Academy of Integrative Medicine (A.A.I.M.).

  • Integrative medicine

    Diplomate in Integrative Medicine, American Academy of Integrative Medicine (A.A.I.M.). Integrative medicine means combining conventional care with evidence-based complementary treatment.

    Diplomate in Anti-Aging Medicine, American Academy of Anti-Aging Medicine (A.A.A.A.M.).
