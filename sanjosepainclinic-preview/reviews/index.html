Why we show the source

Why we name the source

We looked at what the other San Jose auto accident clinics publish about their own ratings, across the six competitor web properties we surveyed on 2026-08-20. Not one of them showed a rating on the page a visitor actually reads. One states "150+ Google Reviews" in its browser tab title and nowhere in the page itself. Another carries a 5.0 from 193 ratings inside the page's structured data, which is code written for search engines rather than text written for you. A number you cannot trace back to its source is not proof of anything. So every figure here carries a platform name and a date, and every review here will name where it was posted.

What we cannot promise

What a review cannot tell you

A review describes one person's experience of this clinic. It is not a forecast of yours. Individual results vary by person, by injury, by how long you waited before you were examined, and by what the testing finds. Two people can walk out of the same collision with the same complaint and need different care for different lengths of time. We do not promise that pain, headaches or restricted movement will resolve, and we will not publish a figure that implies we can. What we will do is tell you what the examination and the on-site testing measured, in plain language, and tell you plainly when a problem is outside what we treat.
