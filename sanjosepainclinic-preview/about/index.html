04 / Why non-surgical, drug-free

What we do and what we do not do

Every treatment offered here is non-surgical and drug-free. We explain the risks of a treatment before care begins, and we say plainly when a case needs imaging, a surgeon or another specialist we do not provide. We do not treat outside our scope, and we do not ask you to stop care your medical doctor has prescribed. Individual results vary from person to person and by condition.

05 / How we work

We assess before we treat

Your first visit is an examination and a history: what happened, where it hurts, what you can and cannot move. If the examination points to nerve or soft-tissue injury, the testing happens here, in a single visit, without an outside referral. That includes electromyography (EMG, a test of how a muscle responds to the nerve feeding it), nerve conduction velocity testing (how fast a signal travels along a nerve), diagnostic vascular studies, diagnostic ultrasound and high-frequency digital X-ray. You get the findings in plain language before a treatment plan is written. Care then runs in stages with a review point at the end of each stage, so both of us can see whether it is working. Individual results vary.
