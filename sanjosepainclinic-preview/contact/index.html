03 / When the door is locked

Office hours.

Two closed days a week

We are closed on Saturday and Sunday. If something feels seriously wrong before we open, go to an emergency room or call 911. An emergency room rules out the injuries that are dangerous today. That is a different job from ours. Ours begins afterwards: measuring the soft-tissue and nerve damage that can turn chronic when nobody tests for it. Book with us for the next working day.

05 / Referral intake

For attorneys.

Referral intake is separate from patient booking. Call (408) 292-2225 and ask for referrals, or send the referral in writing. [UNCONFIRMED]

Send with the referral: client name and date of birth, date and mechanism of collision, carrier and claim number, your letter of protection or lien agreement, and any imaging already taken.

Testing performed on site, in one visit, without an outside referral: electromyography (EMG), which records the electrical activity of a muscle; nerve conduction velocity testing, which measures how fast a signal travels along a nerve; and diagnostic vascular studies.
