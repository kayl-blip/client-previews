Privacy Policy

This notice describes how O'Connor Non-Surgical Pain Clinic collects, uses and protects your personal and health information.

02 / Status of this document

What follows is a draft outline, not a policy in force.

Nothing below is binding. It is a plain-language summary of what each section of the finished notice will cover, published so you can see the shape of it while the reviewed version is prepared.

01Who this notice applies to

Which clinic entity holds your records, and which people, locations and services the notice covers.

02Information we collect

What you give us on intake and insurance forms, what we record during care and testing, and what this website collects on its own.

03How we use your information

Treatment, billing, appointment reminders, and reporting that the law requires.

04Protected health information and HIPAA

HIPAA is the federal health-privacy law. Your clinical records are handled under it, and this section will set out your rights under it, including the right to see your record and to ask for a correction.

05When information is shared, and with whom

The specific situations in which records leave the clinic, and the narrower set of situations in which the law obliges us to release them.

06Auto-accident claims, attorneys and insurers

If you are treating under a claim, your records normally go to your personal-injury attorney and to the insurer handling the claim. This section will explain what gets sent, what you sign to authorise it, and how to withdraw that authorisation.

07This website: forms, cookies and analytics

What an appointment request sends to us, what is stored in your browser, and how to opt out.

08How long records are kept

Retention periods for clinical records and for information collected through this site.

09Your rights, including your California privacy rights

How to request a copy, a correction or a deletion, and how to ask us to stop a particular use.

10Safeguards

The administrative and technical measures used to protect records, and how you are notified if a breach occurs.

11Changes to this notice

How an update is posted and dated, and how you can tell which version applies to you.
