CONSULTING

Many of our clients have expressed to us over the years that the process of obtaining their California liquor license was not anywhere near as off-putting as the many factors that are introduced with actually owning the liquor license. As a liquor license holder, you now have to ensure that your business operates in compliance with the regulations in your city, county, and state. Unfortunately, many business owners do not realize this until they have actually started running their business after receiving their license. Without fully understanding all of the compliance requirements, some find themselves caught with the rude awakening of thousands of dollars in fines or revocation of their business license, or having their business shut down because they failed to abide by the laws surrounding their license. LiquorLicenseAgents.com removes this scenario entirely for our clients. We provide our expert consulting during every step of the licensing process – including after you have obtained your California liquor license.

We begin this process by establishing direct correspondence with the California Department of Alcoholic Beverage Control and compile and file all the necessary paperwork for each of our clients. We also navigate the many pitfalls of the licensing process through our relationships with those in ABC that actually process all of the applications.

Because of the unique relationships that we have established, we are able to collaborate with our clients and the ABC to obtain such important permits as conditional use permits in even the most competitive markets. In the past, we were able to use our good standing to benefit our clients’ applications by creating and submitting an approval letter, along with the application, from all neighbors and business owners in the area, giving a hearing in the city of the business, working with city officials to obtain proof of conditions, networking with local members of city council, communicating with local police contacts, drafting and maintaining extensions for filing (if applicable), drafting a Radius Map, etc.
