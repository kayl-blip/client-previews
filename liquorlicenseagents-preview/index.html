YOUR TOP CALIFORNIA LIQUOR LICENSE SOLUTION

Welcome to Liquor License Agents! We are a one-stop solution to buying, selling or transferring a California liquor license. We work closely with the California Alcoholic Beverage Control (ABC), the licensing and compliance board in charge of regulating the sale of alcoholic beverages in the state of California. If you plan on operating any business that intends to sell alcoholic beverages in any form, you need a California liquor license. Our offices are experts in the liquor license application process with ABC, as well as in assisting with the purchase or sale of a California liquor license in the open market. Liquor License Agents are the best in the industry of liquor license brokering. Our experienced liquor license agents will assist you each step of the way, saving you the time, money, and stress that is typically associated with liquor licenses.
