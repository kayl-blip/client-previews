SCLERAL LENSES IN SPRING, TEXAS

Scleral lenses are ideal for patients with vision problems that can't be corrected by regular contact lenses or glasses. They're larger in diameter than traditional contacts and are designed to vault over the entire cornea, so they fit more securely and comfortably over corneal irregularities.

Scleral lenses offer clear and comfortable vision for patients with conditions such as keratoconus, high astigmatism, and severe dry eye syndrome, as well as patients who have undergone a corneal transplant.

MYOPIA MANAGEMENT IN SPRING, TEXAS

Myopia can increase the risk of serious eye complications later in life if left untreated. That's why we use a variety of proven techniques to help manage the condition, including multifocal contact lenses, atropine eye drops, and orthokeratology (Ortho-k lenses).

We will work closely with you and your child to determine the best course of treatment for their individual needs, ensuring they receive the personalized care they deserve.
