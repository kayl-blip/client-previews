Evidence, and its limits

What we can prove

  • Licence DC-30427
  • NPI 1730196569
  • In practice since 1994
  • In Roseville since 2007
  • One practitioner throughout

Both records can be checked without asking us for anything. Those are the claims on this site that carry a receipt.

Continuity is part of it. The nearest competitor in this market runs two practices behind one phone number; here, the person who assesses you at the first visit is the person you see at the tenth. Read about Dr. Van Wagenen.

What we will not claim

No star rating, and no review count, anywhere on this site.

No outcome percentages for the laser we use.

No cure language, on any page.

A widely-repeated rating figure for this practice traces back to a page that no longer exists, so it is not carried forward. The outcome figures circulating for that laser come from a press release, and the manufacturer publishes none.

Applied kinesiology examination should enhance standard diagnosis, not replace it, and quotes from patients are experiences rather than evidence.
