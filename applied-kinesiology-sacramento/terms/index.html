What this site is

An information site for a single chiropractic practice in Roseville. It describes the method used, the conditions seen, the practitioner, and how to get in touch. It is not a portal, it holds no account and it stores nothing about you between visits.

Not medical advice

Everything published here is general information. It is not a diagnosis, not a treatment recommendation for your situation, and not a substitute for being examined by a provider who has actually looked at you. Reading this site, or sending a message through it, does not make you a patient of this practice. That relationship begins at an appointment, after consent. The healthcare disclaimer sets out the clinical boundaries in full.

No prices and no offers

No fee, rate, package or price appears anywhere on this website, by decision of the practice. Nothing here is an offer capable of acceptance, and nothing here forms a contract for care. If you are uninsured or not using insurance, you have a federal right to a written estimate before care is scheduled, and the good faith estimate page explains how to exercise it.

Booking and enquiry requests

The practice has no online scheduler. A request sent through this site is a request to be contacted, not a confirmed appointment and not a reserved time, and it carries no guarantee of a reply. An appointment exists only once the office has agreed one with you directly. Do not use this site for anything urgent: in an emergency, call 911 or go to an emergency department.

What we ask of you

Give accurate information when you contact us, because a reply is only as good as what it is answering. Do not use this site to send anything unlawful, to impersonate somebody else, or to attempt to interfere with how it runs. Do not send detailed health information through a web form; bring it to the appointment.

Content and other people's marks

The text, illustrations and layout of this site belong to the practice. You are welcome to read them, print them and share a link. Copying the content to publish elsewhere as your own is not permitted.

Product names that appear here belong to their owners and are used only to say accurately what equipment is in the room. FX-635 and Erchonia are the manufacturer's marks, not this practice's, and their use on this site is descriptive rather than an endorsement in either direction.

Links off this site

Some pages link to outside sources, such as the federal registers where the licence and the device clearances can be checked. Those sites are not run by this practice and are not under its control. They are linked because they are the authority on the fact being cited, which is also why a link to one is not an endorsement of everything else on it.

Availability and changes

The site is provided as it is. It may be unavailable at times, and pages are added, corrected and removed as the practice changes. Where a correction matters, the page it is on will say so rather than change quietly.

What is not settled yet

A complete terms document normally ends with clauses on governing law and venue, limitation of liability, indemnity, warranty disclaimers and how disputes are resolved. Those clauses are not written here. The practice has not taken a position on them and no counsel has drafted them, so publishing invented versions would be worse than publishing none.
