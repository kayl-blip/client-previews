Sacramento Applied Kinesiology, Roseville

This is the Roseville chiropractic practice that uses applied kinesiology to find why the pain keeps coming back. One practitioner, one office on North Sunrise Avenue, and a method that starts by asking where the problem is actually coming from.

What makes this practice different from the ones nearby

The honest version of the difference is a capability claim, not a boast, and it is checkable on the competing websites in an afternoon.

Within a few miles of this office there are practices with serious equipment: laser, decompression, red-light. None of them publishes any applied-kinesiology capability. Further out there is a practice with far deeper applied-kinesiology credentials than this one. It publishes no laser, no PEMF and no device stack of any kind. Sacramento Applied Kinesiology is the practice in that captured set running both: a whole-body applied-kinesiology assessment and an FDA-cleared device stack, under one licensed practitioner. The method decides what to treat, the equipment treats it, and the same person does both.

Where to go next

In his own words: "Since moving to Roseville in 2007, my patients and this community have become like family to me." In a single-practitioner practice that is not a marketing line, it is the operating model. Everything below is a way of judging the person you would actually be seeing.

Both are public records, both can be verified without asking this practice for anything, and no competitor in the captured local set publishes a licence number at all. In practice since 1994. That figure comes from the practice's own record and no other.

Whether this is the right place for you

This practice is set up for adults in Roseville and the Sacramento corridor with persistent pain who have already tried a doctor, physical therapy or another chiropractor without lasting relief, and who want to know what is causing it before agreeing to medication or surgery. If that is you, read the practitioner page and then the credentials page.

What we will not claim

No permanent-fix promise, no guarantee, no timeframe, no success rate, no award, no press. Applied kinesiology supports standard diagnosis; it does not replace it.
