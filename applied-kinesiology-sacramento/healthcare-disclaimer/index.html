The scope of practice

A Doctor of Chiropractic in California is a licensed health care provider whose practice covers examination, manual treatment of the spine and joints, and the supporting therapies within the chiropractic scope. It does not include prescribing medication and it does not include surgery. A chiropractor may recommend that you see another provider, and here that recommendation is made whenever the problem is outside what this practice can properly address.

Nothing on this website should be read as a claim to a specialty, board certification or diplomate status that this practice does not hold.

Applied kinesiology is a method, not a credential

Applied kinesiology is used here as part of the examination. Manual muscle testing is one of the tools the practitioner uses to work out where a problem is coming from, alongside history taking and a structural examination. It is a method of assessment.

It is not a qualification, and it is not described on this site as one. This practice holds no applied kinesiology board certification, no diplomate status and no membership claim in any applied kinesiology college. Where other practices hold those credentials, they say so; this one does not have them and does not imply otherwise. The only doctoral title used on this site is Doctor of Chiropractic, which is the qualification actually held. No kinesiology degree is claimed here, because no such degree exists.

The non-diagnostic boundary

The International College of Applied Kinesiology states the limit plainly, and this practice works within it: applied kinesiology procedures are not intended to be used as a single method of diagnosis, and applied kinesiology examination should enhance standard diagnosis, not replace it.

In practical terms, that means muscle testing is never used here to diagnose a disease, to detect an allergy, to assess an organ, or to stand in for medical investigation. If your problem needs imaging, laboratory work or a medical opinion, you will be told so and referred rather than tested.

What is not offered here

This practice does not prescribe or dispense prescription medication, does not perform surgery, and does not offer care for attention deficit disorders, allergies, asthma or ear infections. Four pages making claims in those areas were removed from this site during the rebuild because they sat outside the boundary described above.

No treatment offered here is presented as a cure, and no outcome is guaranteed.

Devices used in care

The practice uses the FX-635, a low-level laser manufactured by Erchonia which operates at 640 nm and is cleared by the United States Food and Drug Administration under K190572 and K180197. A clearance means the device may lawfully be marketed for the indications in that clearance. It is not evidence that the device will work for you, and this site publishes no outcome percentages for it or for anything else.

Where you see a figure or a statistic elsewhere on the internet attached to this device, check whether it comes from the manufacturer. The manufacturer publishes none.

This website is not medical advice

The condition pages, service pages and answers on this site are general information written for people trying to work out whether to come in. They are not a diagnosis, not a treatment plan, and not a substitute for being examined. Reading them, or sending a message through this site, does not create a patient relationship. That begins at an appointment, after consent.

Emergencies

Do not use this website, its forms or its phone line for a medical emergency. If you have chest pain, difficulty breathing, sudden weakness or numbness, loss of bladder or bowel control, a serious injury, or any other condition that may be life threatening, call 911 or go to an emergency department.

Individual results

People respond differently to care, and two people with the same complaint often need different plans and reach different outcomes. Any patient account published on this site describes one person's experience. Testimonials presented apply only to the individuals depicted, cannot be guaranteed, and should not be considered typical.
