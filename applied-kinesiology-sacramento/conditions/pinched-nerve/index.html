Pinched Nerve Treatment in Roseville

Numbness, pins and needles, or a shooting line of pain that follows the same route every time.

A nerve can be caught where it leaves the spine, or anywhere along the path after that. Most people who reach us have already tried anti-inflammatories, physical therapy or another chiropractor. The job is to find where it is actually caught, without drugs or surgery.

Book an appointment

Why it keeps coming back

The pain draws a map, but not of itself

The usual route is sound: your GP, anti-inflammatories or a steroid course, an MRI if it does not settle, physical therapy, and a spinal or orthopedic referral if there is weakness. When a nerve really is compressed at the root, that route is the right one.

What it is not built to do is check the rest of the nerve. Pain that follows a nerve tells you which nerve, not where it is caught. A root that images cleanly can still be irritated further down, at a shoulder, a hip or a forearm that has been quietly taking too much.

Loss of strength, loss of bladder or bowel control, or numbness in the saddle area is an emergency. See a physician immediately.

How we assess it

We test the whole run of the nerve

Muscle testing gives us a way to load each muscle a nerve supplies and find where the signal thins out.

  • The spinal segment the nerve leaves from, and the joints either side of it.
  • Every muscle along that nerve, tested one at a time rather than as a group.
  • The narrow points on the way: shoulder girdle, hip, elbow, forearm.
  • Reflexes and sensation, so the picture is never built on muscle testing alone.
  • Standard orthopedic and neurological screening alongside all of it.

Applied kinesiology is an assessment method, not a diagnosis. It runs alongside standard examination and any MRI or specialist opinion you already have, never in place of them.

What muscle testing is not

It does not diagnose a disc, name a level, or replace an MRI. It narrows down where to look and what to treat first.

What treatment involves

Take the pressure off, then keep it off

The assessment decides which end of the nerve gets worked on first.

Adjusting

Specific work at the segment the nerve leaves, and at whichever narrow point the testing flagged, using Diversified, Sacro-Occipital, Activator or Percussion technique.

Low level laser

Where it is indicated, the FX-635 low level laser: an FDA-cleared, non-thermal 640 nm device (clearances K190572 and K180197). It is cleared for pain relief, which is not the same as a promise about your result.

PEMF and nerve glides

Pulsed electromagnetic field therapy where indicated, plus the glide and strengthening work that stops the same point closing down again.

Under one roof matters here: within three miles of this office the instrumented practices publish no applied kinesiology, and the nearest one that does is twelve miles away with no laser or PEMF listed.
