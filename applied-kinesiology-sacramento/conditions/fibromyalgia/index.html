Fibromyalgia Care in Roseville

Pain that moves, sleep that does not restore, and a fatigue that nobody can photograph.

Nothing here replaces the diagnosis or the medication you already have. This is the physical and nutritional side of the load, examined properly.

Most people who reach us have already tried a GP, a rheumatologist or another chiropractor. The job is to find what is actually adding to it, and to work on that without drugs or surgery.

How applied kinesiology works

Why it keeps coming back

A whole-body problem meets a one-region appointment

The usual route is a long one: your GP, blood work to rule other things out, a rheumatology referral, then medication aimed at pain and at sleep. That work matters, it is where a diagnosis comes from, and this office does not compete with it.

What a short appointment cannot easily do is examine the whole load at once: how you sleep, what you eat, what your neck and mid back have been holding, and what your week has been doing to you. Those things are examinable, and they are rarely all examined together.

Nothing on this page is a claim to treat or reverse fibromyalgia. It is supportive care, alongside your medical management.

How we assess it

One appointment that looks at the whole load

Muscle testing is how we work out which parts of that load are holding and which are not.

  1. History across the whole picture: sleep, digestion, mood, medication, and what a bad week actually looks like.
  2. Standard orthopedic and neurological screening, so nothing treatable gets missed.
  3. Manual muscle testing region by region, separating the sore areas from the ones failing quietly.
  4. A nutritional review, because for some people that is where the biggest unexamined load sits.
  5. Findings said out loud, including what they point to and what they do not.

Applied kinesiology is an assessment method, not a diagnosis. It runs alongside your rheumatology and primary care, never in place of them.

Care is paced to what you can tolerate. A flare after treatment is a signal to do less, not to push harder.

What treatment involves

Low force first, and less of it than you expect

The assessment decides the dose, and with fibromyalgia the dose is usually small.

Gentle adjusting

Low-force work using Activator, Sacro-Occipital or Percussion technique rather than a heavy thrust, aimed only at the joints the assessment flagged.

Low level laser

Where it is indicated, the FX-635 low level laser: FDA-cleared, non-thermal, 640 nm (clearances K190572 and K180197). Cleared for pain relief, which is not a promise about your result.

Nutrition and PEMF

Nutritional counseling where the history points that way, and pulsed electromagnetic field therapy where indicated. Dr. Van Wagenen is a board-certified clinical nutritionist.

Under one roof matters here: within three miles of this office the instrumented practices publish no applied kinesiology, and the nearest practice that does is twelve miles away with no instruments listed. A whole-body assessment is what stops you repeating a treatment aimed at the wrong thing.
