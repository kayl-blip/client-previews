What applied kinesiology is

Why sciatica keeps coming back

The standard route is well worn and most people have already been down it. A doctor's visit, an anti-inflammatory or a muscle relaxant, an X-ray or an MRI, physical therapy, then a referral to an orthopaedic surgeon if it drags on. That sequence is aimed squarely at the segment where the nerve is being compressed, because that is what the scan shows.

It is a reasonable sequence and is not being argued with. It is incomplete when the compression is a symptom of how the rest of you loads that segment. Hips that do not extend, a pelvis sitting rotated, a glute that stopped firing after an old injury: each puts the same disc or piriformis under pressure again once the flare settles.

Which is why the leg quietens for a month, then lights up after a long drive.

How we assess it

Most people booking for sciatica are on a second or third attempt, some after repeated low back adjustments that gave short-lived relief. Manual muscle testing checks how specific muscles hold under load, across the whole body rather than only where the pain is, and narrows a long list to the few things worth treating first. For sciatic pain that means:

  • hip extension and rotation, and the deep glute muscles the nerve passes through
  • how the pelvis is sitting and whether it is loading one side harder
  • the abdominal and low back muscles that should be sharing the job

Alongside standard diagnosis, never instead of it

Applied kinesiology examination is used to enhance standard diagnosis, not to replace it. If your leg symptoms need imaging or a surgical opinion, you will be told. Read the honest answer on muscle testing for the limits.

What treatment involves

A course of care starts from the examination, not from a template. Chiropractic adjustment where the joint work is indicated, then treatment of whatever the muscle testing showed was not holding its share. Where it helps, cold laser or PEMF therapy is used alongside the manual work, in the same room, on the same visit.

Sciatica care built on a whole-body assessment is there to find what is actually driving the leg pain, so you stop repeating a treatment aimed at the wrong thing.

What you will not be promised

No visit count before an examination, no success rate, no timeframe. Sciatica varies too much between people for any of those to be honest. What you can expect is to be told what was found and what is being treated because of it.
