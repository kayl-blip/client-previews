If the question is urgent, or it is a medical emergency, do not use this form. Call 911 or go to an emergency department. Nothing on this website is medical advice and no message sent through it creates a patient relationship. Your first visit explains what does.

Who is actually at the other end

The same practitioner has run this practice since 1994 and has been in Roseville since 2007. When you ring, nobody is triaging you into a queue, and no answering service is standing between you and the person who would treat you.

That continuity is the point. In a category where the nearest comparable practice runs two locations behind a single phone number, one practitioner for thirty-two years is a fact you can check rather than a promise.
