Where patients travel from

Most of the practice comes from Roseville itself, and from Rocklin and Granite Bay a few minutes up the road.

Beyond that, patients travel in from Citrus Heights, Lincoln and the wider Sacramento corridor.

There is one office and one practitioner. No second location, no satellite clinic, no network of branches with the same name on the door.

That is worth saying plainly, because plenty of local health sites publish a page per town and let the reader assume a clinic in each one. This page is the whole footprint.

Why patients drive past a closer chiropractor

Applied kinesiology is not widely offered here. Within three miles of this office no practice offers it at all, and the instrumented clinics nearby publish no applied kinesiology capability. The nearest practice offering the method is about twelve miles away, and it publishes no laser, PEMF or FX-635 capability of its own.

Method and instruments in the same room

So the reason to make the drive is a combination rather than any single service:

  • a whole-body applied kinesiology examination, used to find what is driving the pain
  • chiropractic adjustment by the same practitioner who did the examination
  • FDA-cleared devices on site, including the FX-635 laser, cold laser and PEMF
  • one licensed practitioner running all of it, so nothing is handed between rooms
