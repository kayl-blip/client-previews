About the method

Applied kinesiology and muscle testing

What is applied kinesiology?

A manual assessment method. Specific muscles are loaded one at a time and the response is used to narrow down which structures are working and which are not, so the treatment that follows is aimed rather than guessed.

Does muscle testing hurt?

No. You hold a position while steady pressure is applied for a second or two. It is effort rather than pain, and it stops the moment you say so.

How is this different from a standard adjustment?

A standard visit usually starts at the joint that hurts. This one starts with an examination of the whole chain, and the adjusting is decided by what that examination found.

Is muscle testing a diagnosis?

No. It is used alongside standard diagnosis, never instead of it. The longer and franker answer is on is muscle testing legitimate.

About cost and insurance

What it costs, and why there is no price list here

This practice is consult-first. What a course of care involves depends entirely on what the examination finds, so a price on a web page would be a guess dressed up as an answer. You are told what is recommended, and what it costs, before anything is booked in.

You are entitled to a Good Faith Estimate in writing before treatment. That is a legal right rather than a courtesy, and the Good Faith Estimate page explains how to ask for one and what to do if a final bill comes in higher.

Insurance: the office has not published which plans it accepts, so please call and ask before your first visit. This line gets replaced once the practice confirms it.

About what this practice does not treat

The boundary, stated plainly

Some of this is a legal boundary and some of it is a judgement call, but all of it is better said before you book than after.

  • No diagnosis of disease, and no claim that muscle testing detects one.
  • No allergy testing and no allergy diagnosis.
  • No care claims for ADHD, asthma or ear infection.
  • No prescription medication and no surgery.
  • No substitute for a medical work-up: applied kinesiology examination should enhance standard diagnosis, not replace it.

Where something needs a physician first, you are told at the first visit and referred. Red flags, a suspected fracture, an infection or anything progressive goes to medicine before it goes anywhere else.
