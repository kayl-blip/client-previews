Who Applied Kinesiology Is For

Not everyone. This page exists to help you decide before you spend an appointment finding out, and it is written so that some readers close the tab and go somewhere more useful. That is a good outcome for both of us.

"I have already seen a chiropractor."

Most people here have. If the relief kept fading and the adjustment kept being redone, that is worth bringing in. The difference here is the assessment that decides what to correct in the first place. Is muscle testing legitimate.

The route you have probably already walked

  1. A doctor.
  2. Imaging that came back more or less fine.
  3. Anti-inflammatories.
  4. Physical therapy that helped for a while.
  5. Possibly another chiropractor, where the relief kept fading and the same correction kept getting repeated.
  6. Now a stronger prescription, or a referral.

The one question this assessment adds

You would like to know what is causing this first.

That is the ordinary patient here. Difficult cases where others have fallen short are the normal work, and the nearest practice offering this assessment is twelve miles away.

01

Who tends to benefit

The patient this practice was built around

You are an adult in Roseville or the Sacramento corridor. Something hurts and has hurt for a while: a low back, a neck, a shoulder, a sciatic line down the leg, a heel worst in the first steps of the morning, a headache nobody has explained.

You have already done the reasonable things, in the reasonable order, and the list above is yours. What none of it did was hold. Now you are being offered a stronger prescription or a referral, and you would like to know what is causing this first.

02

Read this part first

Who this is not for

With a red-flag symptom, see a physician now, not us.

  1. Chest pain or pressure.
  2. Sudden weakness, numbness or loss of coordination.
  3. Loss of bladder or bowel control.
  4. A suspected fracture, or pain that began with a serious fall or collision.
  5. Unexplained weight loss.
  6. Fever alongside severe back pain.
  7. Severe night pain unrelieved by any position.

Any of those needs urgent medical assessment first.

Some things this practice does not do at all. No surgery. No prescription medication. No diagnosis of disease. No allergy testing or claims. No diagnostic claim for ADHD, asthma or ear infections, so this site has no pages on them.

Applied kinesiology is not a replacement for your medical work-up. If you need a scan, a referral or a prescription, that is what you should get, and you will be told so.

03

What the assessment covers

What the assessment actually covers

A first appointment covers your history, what you have already tried and what happened, a physical examination, a review of any imaging or reports you bring with you, and the applied-kinesiology muscle-testing assessment itself.

What comes out of it is a plan drawn from what this practice actually has: the chiropractic adjustment, FX-635 laser therapy, cold laser therapy, PEMF therapy, nutritional counselling, and hormone therapy management where relevant. Most plans use more than one. You are then reassessed against the same baseline tests as you go, so the plan answers to measured change rather than to a schedule.

04

How it fits with your medical care

We work alongside your doctor, not instead of them

Keep your physician. Bring your imaging, reports and prescription list, because care goes better when both sides work from the same picture. The governing body sets the boundary and this practice states it openly: applied kinesiology procedures are not intended to be used as a single method of diagnosis, and applied kinesiology examination should enhance standard diagnosis, not replace it.

05

Common questions

Questions from people who have been through the standard route

  1. "My scan was normal, so is there anything even to find?"

    Often, yes. Imaging is very good at showing structure and much less good at showing how a body is loading and compensating. A normal scan rules things out, which is worth having. It does not always explain why something still hurts.

  2. "My physical therapy worked, then stopped working. Why?"

    That pattern usually means the exercise was right for the sore area and the sore area was not the whole story. It is the exact situation this assessment is designed for: finding what keeps re-loading the part you have been rehabilitating.

  3. "Am I going to be told to stop my medication?"

    No. Medication decisions belong to the doctor who prescribed it, and nothing here is offered as a substitute for it.

06

What it costs to find out

What it costs to find out

This practice publishes no prices, and you will find no fee and no introductory offer here: what a plan involves depends on what the assessment finds. This is a cash-pay practice, so before care begins you are entitled to a written Good Faith Estimate under the federal No Surprises Act. Call and ask what a first visit involves.

If rung 02 did not rule you out, book an assessment. Still unsure? Call the clinic and describe the problem; you will be told honestly.
