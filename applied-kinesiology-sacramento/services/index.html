Our Treatments in Roseville

This is the Roseville chiropractic practice that uses applied kinesiology to find why the pain keeps coming back. Six treatments run out of one office and one licensed practitioner. Which of them you get is decided by the assessment, not by a package.

Book an appointment Call the clinic

Why this area matters

Most practices have the method or the machines. Not both.

The assessment decides what is actually driving the problem; the equipment is what it gets treated with.

Every part of that claim is checkable on the competing sites themselves.

If you shop the practices around Roseville you will notice the market splits in two, and neither half is hiding it. The clinics with the equipment, the lasers and the decompression tables and the red-light beds, publish nothing at all about applied kinesiology. The practice in this region with the deepest applied-kinesiology credentials publishes nothing about lasers, PEMF or any device stack.

This office runs both halves under one licensed practitioner. That order matters more than the length of the list, because a clinic with instruments and no method has to guess which instrument to reach for.

What is available here

  • Applied kinesiology assessment Manual muscle testing used alongside your history, a physical exam and any imaging you already have, to narrow down where the problem is actually coming from. This is the step everything else follows from.
  • FX-635 laser therapy A non-thermal low-level laser, 640 nm, FDA Class II, carrying clearances K190572 and K180197. No drugs, no needles, no downtime. It matters most for plantar fasciitis.
  • Cold laser therapy The wider category of low-level laser treatment. Light at a power too low to heat or cut, used to support tissue recovery without medication. Distinct from the FX-635, and kept distinct here.
  • PEMF therapy Pulsed electromagnetic field therapy, used as a recovery modality. No nearby practice in the captured local set publishes this capability at all.
  • Nutritional counselling Clinical nutrition from a board-certified clinical nutritionist, for the part of the problem that has to be addressed from the inside.
  • Hormone therapy management The non-prescription side of fatigue, sleep and recovery, coordinated with the physician who prescribes for you.

  • Chiropractic adjustment The licensed practice underneath all of it, aimed by the assessment rather than repeated by default.
